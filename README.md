# Pavel Peretyagin

Materials scientist and engineer working from atoms to machine tools.

I study how hard materials and coatings are made and how they behave in cutting: spark plasma sintering of ceramics and nanocomposites, PVD coatings for cutting tools, acoustic emission diagnostics, machine-learning interatomic potentials (MACE, CHGNet) and physics-informed control of machining.

Leading research scientist, Cand. Sc. (Eng.), 110+ Scopus publications, h-index 26.

### Featured

**[Into the Edge](https://peretyaginmstu-jpg.github.io/into-the-edge/)** zooms from a gold-coated drill bit in your hand down to the atoms of its coating, from 20 cm to 5 Å. The engine is open, and a zoom for another material is one config file. [Code](https://github.com/peretyaginmstu-jpg/into-the-edge)

[![Into the Edge](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/peretyaginmstu-jpg/into-the-edge/main/media/preview.gif)](https://peretyaginmstu-jpg.github.io/into-the-edge/)

Open to research collaborations and to building interactive zooms like this one for other materials and products.
